3.7 Correlation Between Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice

Spearman's correlation analysis revealed a weak positive correlation between knowledge and attitude (ρ=0.315, p=<0.001), a weak positive correlation between knowledge and practice (ρ=0.381, p=<0.001), and a weak positive correlation between attitude and practice (ρ=0.239, p=<0.001). This suggests that higher knowledge is associated with more positive attitudes and better practices.
